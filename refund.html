Refund Policy

Last updated: 1 October 2026

1. Voluntary Contributions

All payments made through the LORD ZORO support page are voluntary contributions. They are not purchases of goods or services. As such, contributions are generally non-refundable.

2. No Automatic Refunds

Once a contribution is successfully processed through Razorpay and a receipt is generated, it is considered final. We do not offer automatic refunds for change of mind.

3. Errors and Duplicate Payments

If you believe a payment was made in error — such as a duplicate charge, accidental payment, or technical glitch — please contact us at @LORD_X_ZORO on Telegram within 7 days of the transaction. Include your payment ID and a brief description of the issue. We will review your case in good faith and, where appropriate, process a refund.

4. Unauthorized Transactions

If you notice a payment on your account that you did not authorize, contact your bank or payment provider immediately, and also notify us on Telegram. We will cooperate fully with Razorpay and your bank to resolve the issue.

5. Chargebacks

Filing a chargeback or payment dispute without first contacting us may result in permanent restriction from using the Page. Please reach out to us first — most issues can be resolved quickly.

6. Processing Time

Approved refunds will be processed back to the original payment method via Razorpay. Depending on your bank, refunds may take 5–10 business days to reflect in your account.

7. Contact

For any refund-related queries, contact us on Telegram: t.me/LORD_X_ZORO
